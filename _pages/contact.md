    <div class="contact-section">
      <h2>Join the Lab</h2>
      <p>
        We are always looking for highly motivated people interested in robotics, control theory, machine learning,
        and human-robot interaction.
      </p>
      <div class="contact-cards contact-cards-2">
        <div class="contact-card">
          <h3>Postdoctoral Researchers</h3>
          <p>
            Candidates with a background in robotics, control, or machine learning can join through fellowships such as
            the C V Raman Post-Doctoral, National Post-Doctoral, or DS Kothari fellowships.
          </p>
        </div>
        <div class="contact-card">
          <h3>PhD and MTech (Research)</h3>
          <p>
            Apply to the Department of Cyber-Physical Systems through the
            <a href="https://iisc.ac.in/admissions/" target="_blank">IISc admissions portal</a>. Applications typically
            open in March–April and September–October.
          </p>
        </div>
        <div class="contact-card">
          <h3>IISc Master's Students</h3>
          <p>
            To do your master's project with the HiRo Lab, visit Prof. Ravi Prakash in his office (Room 319) or write to
            him.
          </p>
        </div>
        <div class="contact-card">
          <h3>Research Staff and UG Interns</h3>
          <p>
            Email the lab with your interests and CV. UG students can also apply through programs such as the
            <a href="https://www.ias.ac.in/" target="_blank">IASc-INSA-NASI Summer Research Fellowship</a>, usually
            announced in October–November. Internships require at least 2 months at the lab for a certificate, and most
            are self-funded.
          </p>
        </div>
      </div>

      <div class="contact-apply">
        <h3>How to Apply</h3>
        <p>
          Write to <a href="mailto:hiro.cps.iisc@gmail.com">hiro.cps.iisc@gmail.com</a>, cc'ing
          <a href="mailto:ravipr@iisc.ac.in">ravipr@iisc.ac.in</a>, with your CV and a short note on your background,
          why the HiRo Lab interests you (a specific project or research direction), and the skills you would bring.
        </p>
        <p>
          <strong>Note:</strong> We receive a large number of applications. If you do not hear back within a week,
          please send a gentle reminder. If you still do not receive a response after that, your application has been
          reviewed but is not a fit for our current openings.
        </p>
      </div>
    </div>
