Ravi Prakash

Assistant Professor, Department of Cyber-Physical Systems, Indian Institute of Science, Bengaluru
Head, Human-interactive Robotics (HiRo) Lab

ravipr@iisc.ac.in

Dr. Ravi Prakash is an Assistant Professor in the Department of Cyber-Physical Systems at the Indian Institute of Science (IISc), Bengaluru, where he leads the Human-interactive Robotics (HiRo) Lab. His research develops provably safe, sample-efficient learning and control for robots operating in uncertain, contact-rich environments, spanning imitation and reinforcement learning, compliant and optimal control, and the grounding of foundation models for robot manipulation and physical human–robot interaction, with applications in assistive robotics and intelligent manufacturing. He earned his Ph.D. in Control and Automation from IIT Kanpur and held an ERC-funded postdoctoral position in the Learning and Autonomous Control group at TU Delft. He is a 2022 DAAD AInet Fellow, co-author of the survey Interactive Imitation Learning in Robotics (Now Publishers, 2022).
