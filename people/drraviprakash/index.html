Ravi Prakash

Assistant Professor, Department of Cyber-Physical Systems, Indian Institute of Science
Head, Human-interactive Robotics (HiRo) Lab

ravipr@iisc.ac.in

I am an Assistant Professor in the Department of Cyber-Physical Systems at the Indian Institute of Science (IISc), where I lead the Human-interactive Robotics (HiRo) Lab. My research develops provably safe, sample-efficient learning and control for robots operating in uncertain, contact-rich environments, spanning imitation and reinforcement learning, compliant and optimal control, and the grounding of foundation models for robot manipulation and physical human–robot interaction, with applications in assistive robotics and intelligent manufacturing.

I earned my Ph.D. in Control and Automation from IIT Kanpur, advised by Prof. Laxmidhar Behera, and then held an ERC-funded postdoctoral position with Prof. Jens Kober in the Learning and Autonomous Control group at TU Delft. I am a 2022 DAAD AInet Fellow and a co-author of the survey Interactive Imitation Learning in Robotics (Now Publishers, 2022).
